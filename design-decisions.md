# FÆBRIQ Design Decisions

## Cap

The cap front is locked as the centered **FÆBRIQ wordmark in Instrument Serif**, with a short pride circuit stripe centered directly beneath the wordmark. The stripe is not a full-width line and does not sit above the mark. There is no phrase on the cap front.

The cap is the **OTTO Cap 18-253**, black, one size, fulfilled through **Printify Choice using DTF**. The accepted physical sample used this same DTF / Printify Choice configuration; embroidery is not an approved substitute.

The pride circuit stripe uses six equal blocks in this left-to-right order:

`#E8271C` `#F47B20` `#F9D01F` `#009A44` `#0057A8` `#742B8C`

## Tees

All tee designs use **Instrument Serif only**. The phrase is centered on the garment, and the pride bar is a full-width bar pinned to the bottom edge of the design. The bar uses six equal blocks with the same locked hex values and consistent spacing and placement across products.

## Error 404 Tee

The design uses a massive `404` in Instrument Serif at approximately 90% white opacity, with the small italic phrase `Straight Not Found` beneath it and the full-width pride bar pinned to the bottom edge.

## Code It. Serve It. Tee

The phrase is centered in Instrument Serif, with the full-width six-block pride bar pinned to the bottom edge.

## Product titles

The approved product titles are `Deploying Identity v2.0` for the crewneck sweatshirt, `Off The Clock. Still Iconic.` for the hooded sweatshirt, `Code It. Serve It. Rainbow Pride Tech T-Shirt` for the tech tee, and `Error 404: Straight Not Found Tee` for the 404 tee.
