The brand's signature pride bar — six equal blocks in one row, left to right: coral, amber, yellow, green, blue, purple. Use to separate sections, underline the wordmark, or cap a hero.

```jsx
<CircuitRule />
<CircuitRule width={280} />
<CircuitRule height={8} gap={0} />
<CircuitRule width={340} align="right" />
```

- This is the load-bearing graphic device — use it sparingly and deliberately, not as routine decoration.
- Six equal segmented blocks is the confirmed style (June 2026). It reproduces faithfully at any size, including small print. Never substitute a CSS gradient or a smooth rainbow sweep.
- `height` sets the bar thickness and `gap` the space between blocks; `gap={0}` gives one continuous bar.
- The colors come from the `--fae-circuit-*` tokens and are fixed — never recolor, reorder, or drop a block.
