# FÆBRIQ — Etsy Channel Rules (placeholder, shop not yet live)

Status as of 2026/09/27: no Etsy shop exists yet. This file is a rules
placeholder so a future session does not improvise brand or gate decisions
when the shop goes live. Update the status line the day the shop is created,
and remove this notice once it is.

## Relationship to Shopify
Same brand, second channel. Not a separate business, not a rebrand.

## What is allowed to differ by channel
- Catalog and SKU selection. Etsy rewards long-tail, micro-niche, gift-framed
  listings over a broad catalog. Test cheap/fast SKUs (stickers, existing art)
  before designing net-new products for Etsy specifically.
- Etsy SEO titles and tags: keyword-forward, gift-occasion framing, Etsy's own
  search conventions. Does not need to match Shopify title style.
- Pricing structure: Etsy fees differ from Shopify, price per channel, do not
  assume parity.

## What must NOT differ by channel (brand facts carry over unchanged)
- Wordmark: sans FÆBRIQ wordmark with the six-block bar at 1.35x its width,
  on near-black (#0f0e0c bg / #e0e0e0 text).
- Phrase lockups (product prints) keep Bodoni Moda.
- One pride-circuit accent per product.
- Voice: dry, deadpan, terminal wit, with a hook and confident energy, never
  flat or bored-technician delivery.
- No embroidery claims (cap fulfillment is DTF, unverified as embroidery).
- No em dashes or en dashes in any store copy, listing text, or output.

## Hard gates (same as Shopify, still apply)
- No new products, price changes, or listings live without explicit
  instruction.
- No purchases or sample orders without checkout-screen confirmation.
- No Printify catalog changes that could cross-affect the Shopify channel
  without saying so first.
- Do not let an Etsy SEO-title habit bleed into Shopify listings, or a
  Shopify listing style get pasted onto Etsy. Each channel keeps its own
  title/tag convention; brand facts above are the only shared constraint.

## Fulfillment
Assume same Printify pipeline as Shopify unless a channel-specific
fulfillment need is stated. One production pipeline over two, where possible.

## Open questions (resolve when shop is created)
- Etsy shop name/handle.
- Which SKUs launch first (recommend stickers, lowest cost/effort, matches
  Etsy's impulse-buy behavior).
- Whether Printify listings get duplicated per channel or shared.
