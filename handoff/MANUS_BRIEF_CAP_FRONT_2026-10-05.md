# Cap front-on shot: brief (2026-10-05)

One extra angle for the FÆBRIQ cap. The left and right angles already delivered (cap-left.jpg, cap-right.jpg) clip the lockup: the left view cuts the F, the right view cuts the Q and the last bar segments. This shot must show the whole lockup, readable.

## Deliver

- 1 image: the cap straight on, dead centre, lens level with the front panel. Yaw 0 degrees. A tilt of up to 5 degrees is fine if the whole lockup stays flat to camera.
- 2048 x 2048 px, JPG or PNG. Keep the same product-only look as the two delivered angles.
- Pure white seamless background, soft contact shadow under the cap. A clean cutout comes later.
- Same black cap, same dad-style profile, same brim curve and eyelets as cap-left and cap-right. Match them, do not restyle.
- No model, no hands, no props, no extra text, no watermark.

## The print (do not change it)

- Sans FÆBRIQ wordmark, off-white, as in cap-left / cap-right. Check the Æ ligature: it must read F-Æ-B-R-I-Q, six characters, nothing added or dropped.
- Directly under the wordmark, a six-block bar in this order, left to right: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple. All six blocks visible.
- The bar is 1.35x the width of the wordmark, centred under it. The bar extends past the wordmark equally on both sides.
- Lockup sits on the front panel, centred. The centre seam may run through it, as in the delivered angles.

## Do not

- Do not make the print look embroidered: no thread texture, stitch edges, or raised relief. The cap is DTF (printed), and embroidery is unverified.
- Do not add a second logo, tag, label, patch, slogan or any other text anywhere on the cap.
- Do not recolour the cap or the bar.
- Do not touch Printify, Shopify or any account. Generation only.

## Acceptance checklist (report each as met / not met)

1. Full lockup visible, nothing clipped at the panel edges.
2. Wordmark reads FÆBRIQ, six characters.
3. Six bar blocks, correct order, bar about 1.35x wordmark width.
4. No embroidery look, no extra text or marks.
5. 2048 x 2048, white background, no model.
6. Cap matches the delivered left/right angles.

If any item is not met, say which one and by how much. Do not silently substitute.
