# FÆBRIQ — Full Brand Brief

CONCEPT: "Identity as code." Premium queer + developer streetwear. Dark-tech minimalism.
FOUNDER: solo, Toronto-based, queer developer building the brand they wanted to exist.
MARKET/FULFILMENT: sells to the US, US-printed via Printify + Shopify (faebriq.com). Currency USD.
AESTHETIC: all-black apparel; slim hard-edge 6-stripe Pride Circuit bar; Playfair serif primary + geometric sans utility mark.
VOICE: wry, insider, understated-premium, dev + queer in-jokes.
VALUES: queer-founded, year-round (anti "rainbow capitalism"), "costly allyship."
PRODUCTS (Vol.1): Deploying Identity v2.0 Tee + Crewneck; Off The Clock. Still Iconic. Hoodie; Code It. Serve It. Tee; Please Hold Tote (black, white print); Circuit Cap (embroidered, sans wordmark + thin bar); Sticker Bundle.
GO-TO-MARKET: organic-first, niche (queer/dev). Short-form video (TikTok/IG Reels), 4 content pillars (identity-as-code, behind-the-scenes/founder, product-in-context styling, community). 90/10 rule in communities (Reddit, Discord, Out in Tech, IRL Toronto). Realistic: first sales 4-8 weeks, steady 3-6 months.
DO NOT USE: terracotta #C45500 / "MKGH" palette / Etsy — retired.
