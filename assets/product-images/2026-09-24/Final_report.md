## Required disclosure: sticker sheet reconstruction

No dedicated sticker-sheet print file exists in the repository. Slot 3 was reconstructed from five exact single-sticker print files in assets/print-art/sticker-final-system-2026-08-27/: 404 Straight Not Found v3, It's Not a Bug. It's Me., Code It. Serve It., Deploying Identity v2.0, and Please Hold, I'm Rebranding My Identity. Each design appears intact on its own sticker in a 2-col x 3-row grid with the sixth cell empty. The sheet backing is near-black (#101010) rather than white: the five artworks are white/light designs, and a white backing would render them invisible. This matches the brand's dark product photography and keeps every design legible.

## Sticker presentation note

The first sticker build used an opaque white vinyl backing, which made the light artwork illegible. The four established single-sticker products on the live store show stickers as white-print decals applied directly on dark surfaces (transparent vinyl look). All 404 sticker images were rebuilt to match that house style. No white backing appears in any final sticker file.

## Repair history (for the record)

First build failed 7 files on inspection: three macro crops cut into lettering, three sticker files used illegible white backing, the sheet was white-on-white with scrambled designs. All 7 were rebuilt and re-inspected. Minor flag: sticker slot 4 has a mild photographic sheen in one corner (brightness 56 vs 2 to 42 across the rest of the set). Cosmetic, does not affect legibility.
