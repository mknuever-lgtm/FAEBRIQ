# FÆ mascot spec

Last updated: 2026-09-30

## Identity (locked 2026-09-28)
- Black denim golem, soft plush proportions, patchwork panels with frayed edges.
- Rainbow seam stitching in the six Pride Circuit colors. Echoes the cap's rainbow bar; the real cap is DTF printed, never call it embroidered.
- Silver button eyes. Renders so far show jeans-style shank buttons (domed center, ring). Keep that look consistent.
- Denim-patch brows, angled down (scheming, not angry).
- Rainbow band mouth, wide grin, six colors left to right.
- Pointed frayed ears.
- Dark grounds only, no glow, no badge. The face carries the pride flex; the merch stays slim.

## Voice (direction revised 2026-09-30, final voice not yet picked)
- Voiceover only. Mouth stays static. Lip-sync is a later experiment, not launch.
- Third person always ("FÆ searched your wardrobe"). A creature, not the founder in a costume.
- Adult creature voice, never a child. Mid-to-low pitch, dry rasp, smoky whispery edge. Sly old imp.
- Smug, conspiratorial, delivered with a smirk. Slow sly pauses, then a sharp snap on the punchline.
- Banned: sweet, cute, singsong, bright, bouncy, kids-show cheer. Also banned in prompts: "light and nimble", "playful", "cartoon-character energy" (they steer voice tools toward children's TV).
- Test: if a take could narrate a kids' cartoon, delete it. The right one sounds like it is up to something and enjoying it.

ElevenLabs Voice Design prompt (current):
> An adult creature voice, not a child. Mid-to-low pitch with a dry rasp and a smoky, whispery edge, like a sly old imp. Sneaky, smug and conspiratorial, delivered with a smirk. Slow, sly pauses, then a quick sharp snap on the punchline. Cheeky and wry, never sweet, never cute, never singsong. Clear diction. No sing-song rhythm, no cheerful kids-show tone.

Candidates to generate: (1) prompt as written, (2) more rasp and whisper, (3) nasal, cocky, streetwise, (4) lower and gravelly, still adult.

## Hard rules for mascot content
- Only real products and real prices. No invented slogans, products or features.
- Apparel has no wordmark. Never show a FÆBRIQ-wordmark tee or stack as if it were for sale.
- Wherever the wordmark prints, the bar is six solid blocks at 1.35x the wordmark width. Not a thin gradient line.
- Spelling is FÆBRIQ. AI video tools misspell it (seen: "FÄBRIQ"). Check every end card.
