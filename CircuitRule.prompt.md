The brand's signature divider — six rainbow hairlines with hollow terminal nodes. Use to separate sections, underline the wordmark, or cap a hero.

```jsx
<CircuitRule />
<CircuitRule width={280} />
<CircuitRule nodes={false} gap={4} />
```

- This is the load-bearing graphic device — use it sparingly and deliberately, not as routine decoration.
- `nodes` draws the hollow circuit terminals; turn off for a plain six-line rule.
- Keep traces thin; never thicken into bars or fills.
