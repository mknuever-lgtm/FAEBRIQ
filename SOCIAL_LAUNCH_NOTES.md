## 2026-09-08 — Link the Shop app from every social profile

**When:** after the website is finished and the storefront is live. Not before.

**What:** put a reference to the FÆBRIQ Shop app page on every social profile —
bio link, link-in-bio page, pinned post, wherever that channel's one link lives.

**Why it earns a slot:**

- The Shop app page carries a **Follow** button. Followers get notified on new
  drops. That is a retention channel that costs nothing to run.
- Checkout completes in-app on mobile, with no browser handoff.
- Shop app renders **product data only** — images, titles, prices. No Liquid, no
  theme. It stays correct regardless of what the website theme is doing, and
  theme work does nothing for it.

**Where the link comes from:** Shopify admin → Sales channels → Shop. The channel
settings carry the store's Shop app URL. Take it from there — do not guess or
hand-build the URL.

**Prerequisite — do not skip this:** product imagery has to be fixed first. Shop
app is a pure image grid with no copy to lean on. As of 2026-09-08, four of the
five sticker images do not read as stickers (white text on a dark slab, no
die-cut edge, no scale, no surface), the sticker sheet is dark-on-dark and its
five designs are unreadable at grid size, and product backgrounds alternate
between white and near-black so the grid reads as two different stores. Driving
follows to that grid spends traffic on the weakest asset in the store.

Sequence: fix imagery → publish website → build socials → link the Shop app.
