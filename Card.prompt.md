Flat container surface — hairline border, square corners, zero shadow.

```jsx
<Card>Plain content surface</Card>
<Card accent interactive>Featured, clickable</Card>
```

- Depth comes from the `#141414` fill against `#0d0d0d`, not from shadow.
- `accent` adds the circuit top-edge — reserve for featured/highlighted cards.
- `interactive` brightens the border on hover; pair with an onClick.
