# FÆBRIQ — Visual Identity

## Color tokens
- ink (ground):     #0B0B0D   (garments: #000000 true black)
- text:             #FFFFFF
- Pride Circuit bar (slim, hard-edge, 6 stripes, in order):
  red #E40303 · orange #FF8C00 · yellow #FFED00 · green #008026 · blue #24408E · purple #732982
- RETIRED / DO NOT USE: terracotta #C45500, "MKGH" palette.

## Typography
- Display / primary: Playfair Display (serif) — wordmark, statement phrases, headings.
- Utility / small-format: geometric sans (clean grotesque) — cap embroidery, UI labels, captions.

## The Pride Circuit bar
Thin horizontal bar of 6 equal hard-edged color blocks (small gaps). Slim, understated — never a thick block, never a smooth gradient (gradients don't embroider). Sits under/near the statement text.

## Image / render style (for AI image prompts)
"...matte-black background with subtle texture, dramatic low-key side lighting, deep soft shadows, delicate rim light, shallow depth of field, crisp macro focus, moody high-end e-commerce hero, photorealistic, 8k, no text overlays, no watermark."
Per product aim for ~6 images: 1 clean product, 2 on-model, 2 lifestyle (dev desk / queer art-market / city), 1 macro detail of the print or stitch.
