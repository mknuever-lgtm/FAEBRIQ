# FÆBRIQ voiceover recording runbook: one session, five scripts

Source: FÆ voice script pack, approved 2026/10/01, in the FÆBRIQ Content OS.
One honest flag up front: the approved pack holds **five** production scripts, not seven.
The eleven library rewrites below them are backlog, and the pack itself says their
spoken prices and spoken CTAs must be stripped before any of them is produced.
This runbook covers the five approved scripts only. Nothing here rewrites, adds,
or shortens any script. If you want seven in a session, two backlog rewrites
need that strip pass and your approval first.

## Session rules (locked)

- Voiceover only. FÆ pitches the product, Shark Tank style. The apparel is the hero.
- Say "Fay" out loud, every time. One syllable, rhymes with day. Display copy stays FÆ.
- No spoken prices. No spoken CTA. The link lives in the caption, the pinned comment,
  and a 1 second end card.
- Delivery: smug, conspiratorial, gossip leaning in. Never oversold, always a grin
  behind it. Third person only. FÆ works for FÆBRIQ, it does not own it.
- Same mic, same distance, same room for the whole session. Do not change the setup
  between scripts. One planned exception: the After hours closer (script 05) is
  recorded off-mic-distance on purpose. That is the only time the setup moves.
- Record all three takes of a script back to back, with no playback mid-script; it
  breaks the flow. Before moving on, mark your gut pick in the checklist. Confirm
  or change the pick on playback once the whole session is done.

## Recording order (and why)

Shortest first so your voice warms up on the easy ones and is settled by the time
the 20 second scripts arrive. The two 20 second scripts stay in their original
publish order.

1. Standup: 14s
2. 404: 15s
3. Clearance bin: 18s
4. Deploying Identity v2.0: 20s
5. After hours: 20s

Total session: 15 takes plus one closer, roughly 5 minutes of recorded audio plus pauses.

## File naming

Save every take as its own file, in this exact pattern:

`fae-vo-<order>-<slug>-take<n>.wav`

- `<order>` is the two-digit recording order above (01 to 05)
- `<slug>` is the short script slug below
- `<n>` is 1, 2, or 3

Example: `fae-vo-02-404-take2.wav`

Pickups (a flubbed line from a specific take, re-recorded) add `-pickup` before the
extension: `fae-vo-04-deploying-identity-v20-take2-pickup.wav`

Standalone lines that belong to no take use `-<linename>` instead of a take number:
`fae-vo-05-after-hours-closer.wav`

## Per-script sheets

### 01: Standup (14s, slug: standup)

- Takes: 3
  - Take 1: straight, deadpan, conversational pace
  - Take 2: lean into the smug on the middle beat
  - Take 3: safety, same as take 1 but a touch slower
- Pacing: fastest script in the session. Three beats. Do not rush the setup beat;
  the joke needs the video-call framing to land first. Hold a half beat of silence
  before the final two-word punchline, and let the punchline land on the print.
- Files: `fae-vo-01-standup-take1.wav`, `fae-vo-01-standup-take2.wav`,
  `fae-vo-01-standup-take3.wav`

### 02: 404 (15s, slug: 404)

- Takes: 3
  - Take 1: straight, side-eye energy
  - Take 2: drier, flatter, let the joke do the work
  - Take 3: safety
- Pacing: four beats. The second beat is the print push-in; give it room, do not
  talk over the reveal. Hold the beat before the closing number gag, then deliver
  it flat. The punchline lands on the print, not on your voice.
- Files: `fae-vo-02-404-take1.wav`, `fae-vo-02-404-take2.wav`,
  `fae-vo-02-404-take3.wav`

### 03: Clearance bin (18s, slug: clearance-bin)

- Takes: 3
  - Take 1: straight
  - Take 2: extra conspiratorial on the middle beat, like telling a friend
  - Take 3: safety
- Pacing: three beats, and the middle beat is the pride-bar reveal. Pause just long
  enough for the light sweep to stop on the bar before you speak again. The closer
  is arms-crossed dry, not triumphant. This is the "pride all year" script; keep
  the anger out of it and let the smug carry it.
- Files: `fae-vo-03-clearance-bin-take1.wav`, `fae-vo-03-clearance-bin-take2.wav`,
  `fae-vo-03-clearance-bin-take3.wav`

### 04: Deploying Identity v2.0 (20s, slug: deploying-identity-v20)

- Takes: 3
  - Take 1: straight, release-notes rhythm
  - Take 2: smugger on the third beat
  - Take 3: safety
- Pacing: four beats, the longest spoken load in the session. Read the version
  number like release notes, one beat per phrase, no rushing. The third beat is
  the smug peak; the closer goes flat and final, no grin in the voice. If any
  line trips you up, it will be this one; that is what take 3 is for.
- Files: `fae-vo-04-deploying-identity-v20-take1.wav`,
  `fae-vo-04-deploying-identity-v20-take2.wav`,
  `fae-vo-04-deploying-identity-v20-take3.wav`

### 05: After hours (20s, slug: after-hours)

- Takes: 3, plus one standalone closer
  - Take 1: straight, late-night energy, lower than the rest of the session
  - Take 2: a touch more mischief on the middle beat
  - Take 3: safety
  - Closer: the closing line on its own, recorded off-mic-distance (the session's
    one planned setup change) as an off-screen line, same tone, slightly lower
    energy. Save as `fae-vo-05-after-hours-closer.wav`
- Pacing: three beats. This is the after-dark episode, so the whole read sits a
  notch lower and slower than the other four. The closer is delivered off-screen
  with FÆ gone from frame, so record it on its own right after take 3 while the
  tone is still in your voice.
- Files: `fae-vo-05-after-hours-take1.wav`, `fae-vo-05-after-hours-take2.wav`,
  `fae-vo-05-after-hours-take3.wav`, `fae-vo-05-after-hours-closer.wav`
