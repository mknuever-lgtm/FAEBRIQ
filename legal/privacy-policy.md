## 1. Information We Collect

**Personal Data** — Personally identifiable information, such as your name, shipping address, email address, telephone number, and demographic information, that you voluntarily give to us when you register with the Site or participate in activities related to the Site. You are under no obligation to provide personal information, however refusal may prevent use of certain features.

**Derivative Data** — Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and pages viewed before and after accessing the Site, used for security, operation, and internal analytics.

**Financial Data** — Data related to your payment method (e.g., card brand, expiration date) collected when you purchase, order, return, or exchange. We store only very limited, if any, financial information — all financial information is stored by our payment processor, Shopify Payments or PayPal.

**Data From Social Networks** — User information from social networks (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest) if you connect your account, including name, username, location, gender, birth date, email, profile picture, and public contact data.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

To create/manage your account; process orders and payments; deliver products and services; email you about your account or order; request feedback; resolve disputes; respond to service requests; send marketing communications; compile anonymous statistical analysis.

## 3. Disclosure of Your Information

**By Law or to Protect Rights** — disclosed as required to respond to legal process or protect rights, property, and safety.

**Third-Party Service Providers** — shared with parties performing services on our behalf (payment processing, data analysis, email delivery, hosting, customer service, marketing). **For our print-on-demand fulfillment model, we share necessary order and shipping information with our print-on-demand partners to produce and ship your custom items.**

**Marketing Communications** — with your consent, shared with third parties for marketing as permitted by law.

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
