## 2026-09-22 - Triple Whale and Postscript: later-stage tools, not pre-launch

**What:** Triple Whale (revenue, orders, AOV, ad spend, and campaign pacing)
and Postscript (SMS list building and automated repeat-purchase flows) both
need an already-launched store with traffic, ad spend, or a visitor/customer
base to be useful. Installing either now is setup work against no data.

**Revisit when:** the store has live traffic, plus one of: running paid ads
(Triple Whale) or enough visitor/customer volume to justify SMS collection
(Postscript).

**Sequence from the research:** validate the product or offer, build the
store, get initial traffic through free or low-cost channels, track basic
funnel events (views, add-to-carts, checkouts, purchases), build an email or
customer list, then layer in analytics or SMS automation.

**Source:** chat research using FAEBRIQ background plus Triple Whale and
Postscript product pages, forwarded into this session 2026-09-22.
